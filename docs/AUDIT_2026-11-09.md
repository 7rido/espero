**Written 2026-11-09 on `liq-breakout` at `283dab6`, by an agent.** The audit itself was read-only; what was fixed the
same day is listed under "Fixed" at the end, and everything else is open. It covers
what `docs/AUDIT_PROD.md` (from `221d111`) never saw: `espero/hl_mainnet.py`, `espero/mf_canary.py`, today's change

mainnet module is **not ready for rung 2** (M1 to M4), and the testnet canary has bugs that will bite at its first
exits, the week of 2026-11-16 (C3, C5, C7, C8).
